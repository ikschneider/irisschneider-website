About Research Speaking Blog

Speaking

I offer keynote presentations on the science of effective decision-making, covering topics including cognitive biases, heuristics, decision-making under uncertainty, and the benefits of ambivalence. I provide recommendations that are insightful and actionable — practical strategies audiences can use to enhance their decision-making.

I also offer coaching, mentoring, and advice on the challenges and opportunities in academia, drawing on my training as a psychologist and my experiences as a first-generation student and female academic across countries and cultures.

Research areas available for talks

Decision-making and bias

How mixed feelings shape the quality of our choices — and why staying "torn" can lead to better, less biased decisions.

"The upside of not making up your mind"

Leadership and organizations

Ambivalent leaders receive more honest feedback — because employees feel safer speaking up to someone who admits complexity.

"Why the best leaders don't always have the answer"

Relationships and trust

Mixed feelings about a partner, colleague, or institution reveal hidden dynamics in how we connect, cooperate, and build trust.

"What your mixed feelings are trying to tell you"
